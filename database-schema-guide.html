PK

Primary Key: unique identifier in a table. Example: user_id.

FK

Foreign Key: connects one table to another. Example: users.role_id.

1 to Many

One record connects to many records. Example: one user can place many orders.

Many to Many

Usually built through a bridge table. Example: products and discounts.

Schema Summary

Your schema is a hybrid e-commerce and motorsport business system. It handles user management, product catalog, inventory, orders, payments, delivery, after-sales support, and a special flow for high-value car purchases.

Core business idea

Users browse products, add items to cart or wishlist, place orders, pay, receive shipment, and possibly review, return, or refund items later.

Car-specific idea

Cars are not treated like normal merchandise. They go through request review, appointment, approval, and a separate car order process.

Design strength

The schema clearly separates operational areas into modules, which is good for scaling, explaining, and maintaining the system.
